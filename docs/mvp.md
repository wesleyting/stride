# Stride — MVP v1

## Core Experience

Stride gives each area you're working on its own space and makes it easy to continue where you left off.

**Core loop:**

> Open an activity → see where you left off → make progress → record what matters → easily continue later.

## Activities

Users can create and manage multiple activities at the same time, such as Guitar, Running, Piano, or Wellbeing.

V1 will use a small set of predefined activity templates rather than a fully customizable system.

Initial template directions:

* **Practice** — skills such as guitar, piano, or singing
* **Journal** — wellbeing, reflection, or other freeform areas
* **Fitness** — running, gym, or other physical activities

Custom block-based activities can be explored later. The underlying system should remain flexible enough to support them.

## Recording Progress

Recording should be quick and should not require every available field to be completed.

**Structure should be available, not required.**

Different activity types can collect different information.

For example, a Practice activity may track what someone is practicing, their goals, current proficiency, and where they left off.

A Journal activity may primarily offer freeform writing with optional structured information such as a personal rating.

## Continuity

When users return to an activity, Stride should help them quickly understand:

* What they were doing
* Where they left off
* What changed
* What still needs attention
* What they previously wanted to do next

The amount of context can adapt to how long it has been since the activity was used.

## Intelligent Context

Stride should turn what users record into useful context without requiring them to manually organize everything.

This can include:

* Summarizing recent activity
* Identifying progress or changes
* Remembering difficulties or unfinished areas
* Recognizing things the user wants to return to
* Providing broader progress summaries over time

AI should primarily work in the background rather than functioning as a chatbot.

> **Be at the ready with helpful context when it's needed.**

## V1 Platform

Stride will begin as a web application.

## Not in V1

* Social features
* Friends or sharing
* Notifications
* Calendar
* AI chatbot
* Custom block/activity builder
* Native mobile app
* Complex integrations

Integrations can be explored later based on what is useful and technically practical.

## MVP Success

The MVP succeeds if someone can use Stride for several different areas of their life, step away from one of them, and return later with enough useful context to continue without having to reconstruct what they were doing.
